Build review

Four sets are left to review: All Content, the landing page template, the two legal pages and Contact. Everything else has been decided. The homepage is the agreed build; on 5 August you picked The Table, Three Doors, The Roster and The Through Line Down; on 7 August Streetlight as the one template for all three solution pages, The Studio for The Empower Podcast and The Dome for Capitol Chat; on 11 August The Pinned Method for EPIC, Five Minutes for Email Sign Up and The Network for Ambassador; and One Screen for Donate. Each decided set shows that one build and nothing else. The twenty-six we showed you that you did not choose are out of the way rather than gone: tick Archived in the filter, or follow the archive link under any set, and every one of them is still there and still building. Every page uses the approved copy from the Website Refresh Roadmap, the brand palette, and the same navigation and footer. What changes is the composition.

This page is a review tool. It is never part of the hand-off.

Donate

  • Chosen: Donate C

    One Screen

    The page has one job, so the job is on the first screen: a short hero on the left, and on the right the only two decisions that cost a donor any clicks, how often and how much. Choosing carries straight into Empower’s own form with the answer already in it, so nothing is stated twice. The form itself is not drawn here: The Card is the reading that reproduces it, and showing it on both made this page about the form rather than about the choice. Everything the first two readings put before the ask now sits after it, for the reader who has not decided yet, and on a phone the choice moves above the headline. Nobody should scroll past an argument to reach the thing they came to do.

    Feels like
    Direct. One decision, in view
    Signature
    Choose first, form fills in below
    Best for
    Fewest clicks between arriving and giving

Landing page template

  • Template A, new

    The campaign kit

    Six independent blocks: a campaign hero with one action, the ask with a three-point summary beside it, an image-and-text pair that can be flipped and run twice, a voice, the action band, and three links out. Delete any of them, reorder them, run one twice — nothing depends on what sits above or below it. The one orange filled button is the campaign’s ask, which is the whole reason this page type exists.

    Feels like
    A page you read down to the ask
    Signature
    Six removable blocks, one navy action band
    Best for
    A case that has to be made before anyone acts

  • Template B, new

    The held ask

    The same campaign, with the ask moved out of the bottom of the page and into a panel that stays beside the argument as you read. One dark screen and it is the first one, with the photograph running off the right edge; then white, with the case in the left column and the form held in the right. On a phone the panel comes first, directly under the hero, because that is where a phone reader will and will not scroll to. For an event, the same panel holds the date, the place and a register button.

    Feels like
    A page with its hand out, politely, throughout
    Signature
    Navy hero, bleeding photograph, sticky ask panel
    Best for
    Deadlines, events, anything with a closing date

Legal pages

  • Document, new

    Privacy Policy

    Empower’s existing privacy policy, word for word, on this build’s header, footer and reading column. Nothing is rewritten and nothing is added. Two structural things did change and both are recorded: the document’s one section heading was an h3 sitting directly under the h1, which skips a level and fails the accessibility sweep, so it is an h2 here; and the paste wrappers WordPress kept from the original Word document are gone. There is no “last updated” line because the document does not state one, and the date WordPress holds records a save rather than a revision.

The same on every page

  • Every headline, section intro and solution promise is the approved copy from the roadmap, used verbatim. Where a variation splits a sentence across a heading and a paragraph, the words are unchanged.
  • Empower Blue, Empower Orange and the brand type. Gotham and Whitney are stood in for by Figtree and Source Sans 3 until the licences arrive.
  • One header and one footer everywhere. The About pages use the same utility strip and plain dropdowns as the agreed homepage.
  • Exactly one orange filled button per page, per the brand’s one-action-per-view rule.
  • WCAG 2.2 AA, reduced motion honoured, working without JavaScript, no sideways scroll at 320px.
  • No text sits directly on a photograph anywhere in this set — every word is on a solid or near-solid surface, so contrast survives the stand-in photography being replaced.
