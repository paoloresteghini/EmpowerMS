The homepage is settled — the agreed build is at the top. Below it are the reference homepages the decision was made from, and then the About Us pages: three ways to read Who We Are and three ways to read What We Do. Every page uses the approved copy from the Website Refresh Roadmap, the brand palette, and the same navigation and footer. What changes is the composition.

This page is a review tool. It is never part of the hand-off.

The same on every page

  • Every headline, section intro and solution promise is the approved copy from the roadmap, used verbatim. Where a variation splits a sentence across a heading and a paragraph, the words are unchanged.
  • Empower Blue, Empower Orange and the brand type. Gotham and Whitney are stood in for by Figtree and Source Sans 3 until the licences arrive.
  • One header and one footer everywhere. The About pages use the same utility strip and plain dropdowns as the agreed homepage.
  • Exactly one orange filled button per page, per the brand’s one-action-per-view rule.
  • WCAG 2.2 AA, reduced motion honoured, working without JavaScript, no sideways scroll at 320px.
  • No text sits directly on a photograph anywhere in this set — every word is on a solid or near-solid surface, so contrast survives the stand-in photography being replaced.
