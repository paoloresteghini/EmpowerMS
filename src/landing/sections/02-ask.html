What we asked for

The Education Scholarship Account gives a child with special needs the option their family judges best. It was funded for fewer children than qualified for it, so the state kept a waiting list — and a waiting list for a child’s education is a year of that child’s schooling.

We asked lawmakers for one thing: fund the accounts every eligible family had already been told they qualified for.

  • What is happening

    Eligible families are approved, then told to wait for a place to open.

  • What it costs

    A school year a child does not get back, in the years that matter most.

  • What would fix it

    Funding that follows eligibility, so the list stops existing.
